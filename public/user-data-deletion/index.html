How to request deletion

Command Center Hub is an internal operations dashboard for Relax Fix UAE. If you have used a Meta-connected feature and want your personal data deleted, email coachaymanmahrous@gmail.com from the address associated with your request.

Include your name, the Meta account or business connected to the request, and a short description of the data you want removed. We will verify the request, remove or anonymize eligible data from our systems, and confirm completion within 30 days, subject to legal and security requirements.

Questions

For deletion questions, contact coachaymanmahrous@gmail.com.
